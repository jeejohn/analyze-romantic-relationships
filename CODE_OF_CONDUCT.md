# Code of Conduct

Contributors must discuss evidence, rules, and software behavior without harassing or exposing real people. Sexist generalizations, humiliation, threats, doxxing, publication of private relationship material, and attempts to recruit the project into targeted accusations are not permitted.

Critique the claim and its evidence, not a contributor's identity or relationship history. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or close discussions that create privacy or safety risk.
